Questions about the data

Every frame is real SPHEREx data, shipped with the site.

Read the data credits

Where do the images come from?

SPHEREx Level 2 spectral images from DR1 and QR3, read from IRSA’s public bucket and saved as static files.

Why does SkyBlink move a coordinate?

Nearby stars drift. WISE 0855 sits 125.7″ from the brief’s position, so SkyBlink applies its CatWISE2020 motion first.

What does Saturated mean in the viewer?

The star overflows the detector core. For RW Aurigae and R Draconis the viewer measures the PSF wings instead.

Why is WISE 0855 marked Blended?

A Gaia star of G 16.3 overlaps it at short wavelengths, so band D6 is its default.

Which wavelengths does SPHEREx cover?

Six detector bands, D1 to D6, run from 0.75 to 5 µm in 102 spectral channels.
