      Where the passes come from

      Each WISE frame is an unWISE time-resolved coadd: the dozen or so exposures of one sky pass stacked into one image. WISE slept from 2011 to 2013, so the dates jump there.

      Why nothing flickers

      All WISE frames of a star share one brightness stretch, so field stars hold steady and only real changes show. The SPHEREx frame uses its own.

      The dots and the dashed line

      Dots are Gaussian fits to the star in each past pass. The dashed line is the path that Gaia DR3 or CatWISE2020 motion and parallax predict.
