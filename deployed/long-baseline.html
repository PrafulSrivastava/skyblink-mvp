    Where the frames come from

    The 2010 frame is a cutout of the WISE All-Sky Atlas at IRSA, dated by the single WISE exposures that cover the star. The SPHEREx frame stacks one visit’s frames whose wavelength at the star falls in the matching window.

    One shared grid

    49 by 49 pixels at 6.15″, centred halfway between the two positions so Barnard’s Star stays in view. WISE’s 1.375″ pixels are averaged into each cell.

    The circles and the arrow

    Predicted from Gaia DR3 or CatWISE2020 motion, not drawn by hand. A Gaussian fit lands within a pixel of them wherever the core is not saturated.
