      Where the frames come from

      IRSA’s Moving Object Search Tool lists every SPHEREx exposure that caught the asteroid. SkyBlink keeps one detector, so all frames share a band, and puts them on one grid by their WCS.

      Why the asteroid carries a flag

      The SPHEREx pipeline marks pixels that disagree with the rest of the visit. A moving rock does exactly that, so SkyBlink shows those pixels and lists the flag.

      Asteroids left out

      Iris and Eunomia saturate the detector in every frame of their best passes. Four fainter near-Earth asteroids, at V 18.5 to 21.6, were not attempted.
